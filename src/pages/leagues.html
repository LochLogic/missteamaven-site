Multi-day & multi-league

Bootcamps & camps

Book Maven for more than one day or more than one league, or invite her to a camp you’re hosting. Think RollerCon, Camp Elite and the North East Derby Convention.

What counts as a bootcamp?

Anything larger than a single-day, single-league clinic. Usually that means multiple days, many leagues, or both.

What topics can you cover?

Anything from blocking to jamming, strategy and communication, basic skills to advanced footwork. I’ll work closely with any other coaches at the camp so all the gaps in the instruction plan are filled.

How big a group can you run?

Private sessions up to full bootcamps with over 100 participants. Sessions are timed and rotated so nobody spends the day waiting around.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the needs and location of your camp, so every package is custom. Tell me about your event and I’ll put options together.
