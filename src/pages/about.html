About Maven

Built by the game. Driven to give back.

Elite experience. Human-centered coaching. More than a decade competing, coaching and obsessing over every layer of roller derby.

My story

Derby has been one of the great loves of my life.

It has pushed me, humbled me, strengthened me, and given me a sense of purpose, identity and community that is hard to put into words.

Over the years I’ve poured myself into learning every layer of the game, from technical skill and strategy to mindset, teamwork and performance under pressure.

Now coaching is one of the most meaningful ways I get to give back. I work with leagues, teams, coaches and individual skaters to build stronger skills, smarter strategy and deeper confidence. Whether I’m leading a clinic, breaking down gameplay or working one-on-one, I want people to leave better prepared and more empowered in their understanding of themselves and the game.

Great coaching should challenge people, support them, and help them see what they are truly capable of.
