How it works

Find the gap. Close it.

I help skaters, teams and coaches figure out what’s actually standing between where they are and where they want to be, then build the training to close that gap.

01

Free intake chat

Tell me what you’re struggling with or what you want to be better at. No need to diagnose it yourself first. That’s what this conversation is for.

02

Decode

Footage, observation and honest questions to find the patterns you can’t see from inside the game. Skill, strategy, mindset or systems.

03

Build

Training built around what we found: on-skates drills, off-skates work, game plans and the accountability to make it stick.
