Coaching the coaches

Better athletes start with better coaching.

Knowing roller derby and knowing how to teach roller derby are two different skill sets. This is virtual education for coaching staffs that want to be more intentional about how they teach, communicate, plan, give feedback and develop athletes.

The goal

Not to make every coach coach the same way. A shared framework, better tools, and more confidence in why you teach what you teach.
