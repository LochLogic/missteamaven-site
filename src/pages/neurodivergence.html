Why it matters

Derby asks a lot of a brain.

Whistles, bodies, instructions, spatial information, physical contact, strategy, emotional regulation, rapid decisions and constant communication, all at once.

For neurodivergent athletes, the way information is taught can make the difference between thriving and constantly feeling like they’re failing.

Not easier. More effective.

This isn’t about making practices easier or lowering expectations. It’s about making coaching more effective for every skater on the track.
