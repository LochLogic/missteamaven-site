Individual junior coaching

Virtual or in person, tailored to your skater’s age, experience, goals, learning style and current needs. Skating skills, derby strategy, position-specific development, footage analysis, confidence, competitive mindset, age-appropriate off-skates work, and preparation for tryouts or higher levels of competition.

Junior team training

Working directly with junior leagues and teams through clinics, bootcamps, coach education, footage review and customized development programs. Past work includes Gotham Girls Junior Derby and Canada Junior Roller Derby.

More than better skating

Winning matters. Development matters more.

My goal isn’t simply to make a junior athlete better at roller derby today. It’s to help them learn how to:

  • Train, and understand why they’re training
  • Compete and communicate under pressure
  • Recover from mistakes and advocate for themselves
  • Build a healthier relationship with being an athlete
Parents are part of the process

Every junior coaching relationship starts with a free conversation with the parent or guardian and the skater, so we can make sure it’s the right fit.
